How the vibe is placed

Valence is P(positive) − P(negative) from the sentiment model. Energy weights the emotion model’s probabilities by where each emotion sits on the circumplex’s arousal axis ({% for e, a in arousal.items() %}{{ e }} {{ a }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}) and nudges it for how it’s typed (exclamation marks, CAPS, elongated words) and for a short list of calm or activated words. Those placements and nudges are hand-set constants, not learned. The word cues exist because TweetEval’s four emotions have no calm-pleasant class, so without them a cosy Sunday reads as high-energy joy. Nothing was trained on “hype” or “chill” labels; the quadrants are a reading of the two real models.
